Do not publish unverified clinical, dermatologist, cruelty-free, shipping, ingredient-completeness or efficacy claims. Resolve the current bio's mixed “ships across India” and “coming soon” wording before treating either as confirmed.

## Operating rules

- Prepare three feed-quality concepts per week; favour 4:5 portrait posts and adapt one into a Reel when source video supports it.
- Use one clear hook, one product or lesson, one call to action, and 3–6 relevant hashtags.
- Reply to genuine comments in the brand voice, but never make diagnostic or medical claims.
- Do not mass-follow, mass-like, copy generic comments, buy followers or send unsolicited DMs.
- Obtain approval immediately before every public post, comment, message, like or profile edit.
- Record reach, views, saves, shares, profile visits, follows and link taps weekly. Optimise for saves, shares and profile visits rather than follower count alone.
