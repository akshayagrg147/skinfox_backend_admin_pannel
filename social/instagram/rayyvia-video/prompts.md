# Built-in image-generation prompts

These five frames were created with the built-in image-generation tool. The existing Rayyvia pack photograph was used as the product reference wherever noted.

## Product hero

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: one key frame for a fast-cut ultra-wide skincare campaign video
Primary request: Create a clean, high-energy product hero for SkinFox Rayyvia Sun Protect inspired by modern Gen-Z beauty advertising, while remaining an original composition.
Input images: Image 1 is the product reference; preserve the photographed Rayyvia 60 g tube exactly.
Scene/backdrop: Seamless white studio with a bold split wash of butter yellow and soft blush pink, tiny translucent citrus-like light reflections, no literal fruit.
Subject: One exact Rayyvia Sun Protect tube standing upright, slightly left of center, crisp and dominant.
Style/medium: Photorealistic premium beauty product photography.
Composition/framing: Very wide landscape with generous white negative space on the right for motion-graphics copy; low camera angle; no cropping of product.
Lighting/mood: Bright, soft, optimistic, glossy but natural.
Constraints: Preserve the package shape, logo, color blocks, cap, and visible label. Add no headline, extra logo, price, claims, badges, or watermark.
Avoid: altered packaging, misspelled text, duplicate tubes, people, hands, medical imagery.
```
